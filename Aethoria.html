A STRATEGY GAMEPLAY IN BROWSER
Left click / drag to select  ·  Right click to command  ·  WASD or arrows to scroll

HOW TO PLAY

Goal: raise an economy, build an army, and destroy every enemy building.

  • Select — left click, or left drag a box. Double click picks all of one type on screen.
  • Command — right click ground to move, trees / berries / gold / stone to gather, enemy to attack, your construction site to help build.
  • Build — select villagers and use the command panel: houses raise population, farms give steady food, storage pits accept wood / gold / stone, barracks train soldiers, towers need stone and the Tool Age.
  • Advance — at the Town Center, pay food to reach the Tool and Bronze Ages for stronger troops.
  • Camera — WASD / arrows / screen edges, wheel scrolls. Minimap: left drag to look, right click to send orders. . finds an idle villager, Space jumps to your Town Center, Esc cancels or opens the menu.
